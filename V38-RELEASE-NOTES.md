# V38 · Another JoJo Update

This release adds three new Stand families across six playable Stand profiles: Mandom, D4C, and Tusk Acts 1 through 4.

## Mandom

- Six-round Bullet economy replaces Energy and Reload replaces Rest.
- Gunslinger doubles Movement Points on the last bullet and empowers the last shot.
- Shoot, real-geometry Ricoshoot, and cylinder-dumping Rapid Fire preserve ordinary on-hit systems.
- Rewind reuses Riftbound's existing combat snapshot authority to restore exactly four combat turns and then enforces exactly a four-turn cooldown, allowing the canonical loop.
- Mandom remains attached to the user rather than becoming an independently movable Stand unit.

## D4C

- Dirty Deeds dynamically trades original maximum HP for living parallel bodies and rewards having no clones active.
- Summon Self and Summon Enemy create real low-HP copied fighter shells with their stats, equipment, items, weapons, and AI identity while preventing recursive clone graphs.
- The fourth living Self triggers a parallel detonation.
- Target imposes a map-wide compulsory destination on every D4C parallel body until it arrives.
- Dojan~ temporarily removes Valentine from ordinary dimensional interaction.
- Love Train reflects ordinary damage for five turns while Causality can penetrate it.

## Tusk

- Acts 1 through 3 use Spin instead of Energy, a separate 10-Nail resource, Crippled movement, and manifested Fibonacci generation from movement, critical attacks, and successful guards.
- Act 1 introduces wall-piercing Nail Shots, Herbal Tea healing/nail regrowth, Spin Punch, and floor-earned Evolve progression.
- Act 2 adds persistent Nail Hole pursuit and an all-Spin Golden Shot.
- Act 3 makes Golden Shot more efficient, adds Wormhole travel and Wormhole Uppercut, and deliberately fails the final ordinary Evolve because the rotation is incomplete.
- On Floor 40, Tusk replaces Sukuna/Mahoraga with a two-phase Funny Valentine calamity fight. Phase 2 revives Valentine at 50% HP with permanent Love Train.
- The first Act 4 awakening requires the completed Act 3 evolution prerequisite, below 50% HP, maximum Spin, exactly one Nail, and a Golden Shot into active Love Train. The shot transforms into Infinite Rotation before Love Train can reject it.
- Act 4 removes Crippled, enables flight, multiplies Movement Points by 3.58, displays infinite Spin, grants unavoidable Causality-level Stand contact, Corrosive Spin, ORA ORA ORA ORA!, CHUMININ!, Infinite Wormhole, and Banish.
- Banish uses explicit Infinite Rotation execution authority below 40% HP rather than numerical overkill, so ordinary death-return rules cannot restore the victim.
- After Valentine, Johnny returns to Act 3 and permanently learns the conditional route back to Act 4 instead of remaining transformed forever.

## Presentation and certification

- Six authored vector Stand portraits are included for battlefield presentation.
- Dedicated resource docks, Love Train walls, wormholes, ricochet indicators, defense tearing, transformation presentation, and V38 cinematic layers are included with reduced-motion support.
- Live Codex totals become 13 Stands, 69 profiles, and 314 displayed techniques while prior historical release views remain separately certified.
- The V38 regression suite covers the new resource economies, rewind loop, real fighter clones, Love Train/Causality authority, Tusk evolution route, Funny Valentine phases, Act 4 awakening, Infinite Rotation execution, and post-battle reversion.
